About FitLife

What We Are Building

FitLife is a single-user health dashboard. When you register, you enter your body stats, fitness goal, diet preference, and activity level. After login, you see one personalized page with everything calculated and selected for you — no generic or demo data.

Secure account

Email login, encrypted passwords, and a private profile only you can access.

Profile-driven plans

Workout and diet content changes based on fat loss, muscle gain, or maintenance — and veg / non-veg / vegan.

Live BMI

BMI is calculated from your height and weight with a clear category and guidance message.

Weight tracking

Log your weight over time and view your personal progress chart.
